The Three Operating Rules

Rules we recruit by. No exceptions.

01

Never fictional jobs

Every opening comes from an authorized employer or a verified listing — source, employer, and date recorded. We do not invent roles to harvest resumes. Ever.

02

Never unreviewable decisions

AI scores are advisory only. A designated human reviewer verifies every exclusion from a completed search's eligible pool before it takes effect — and can reverse it. Candidates can correct their record.

03

Never your employer of record

HireVessa recruits; the employer hires and employs. No payroll, no temp staffing, no employee leasing. And candidates are never charged a fee — not at application, not at placement, not ever.

How it works

A repeatable process. A human hiring decision.

Our service model organizes real jobs and consenting candidates around clear, evidenced requirements—not a promise of instant placement.

From intake to close

Seven steps, with accountability throughout.

01

Position authorization

An employer confirms a real vacancy, its pay range, location, relevant qualifications, and the people authorized to make hiring decisions.

02

Written matching criteria

Job-related requirements are documented and confirmed by the employer before matching begins.

03

Authorized sourcing

Candidate sourcing uses only permitted channels. Outreach identifies the real role and uses appropriate consent and opt-out controls.

04

Evidence-led verification

Relevant qualifications, licences where needed, availability, and candidate permission are reviewed and traced to their source.

05

Human oversight

A a designated human reviewer verifies every exclusion from a completed search’s eligible pool before it takes effect, and can reverse it. Candidates have correction and alternative review pathways.

06

Shortlist and interviews

Employers receive evidence-backed profiles, coordinate interviews, and retain sole authority over hiring.

07

Close and document

Filled or closed roles stop generating contacts. Records follow approved retention and deletion procedures.

AI supports coordination

Matching preparation, authorized sourcing workflows, organizing relevant information, scheduling, and follow-up can be supported by AI under defined human supervision.

People decide who gets hired

AI never becomes the employer, does not make final hiring decisions, and must not make unreviewable consequential adverse decisions. Human review is part of the designed process.

The active-search rule

What counts as an active search?

A search runs from employer authorization until the role fills, closes, is paused in writing, or meets a documented inactivity threshold.

After 45 days without employer engagement and two documented follow-up attempts, the search parks and its slot is freed. Pausing a search does not create a hidden active-work charge.

Privacy · Draft

Privacy starts with purpose.

This page describes the intended data-handling approach for HireVessa. It is a PRELAUNCH DRAFT, not an approved final notice. No candidate or employer information is collected through this site.

Publication status: Draft for legal and operations review. Before live intake, complete the operator identity, verified privacy contact, jurisdictions, service providers, retention exceptions, consumer rights instructions, and applicable state notices.

1. Scope and current website status

HireVessa Recruiting is a planned employer-paid recruiting service operated under Apex Grid Engineering PLLC. This prelaunch static site does not use application forms, analytics, tracking pixels, marketing cookies, or a functional submission endpoint. Browsers and hosting providers may process standard technical request logs according to their own systems.

2. What information may be collected later

With approved intake and appropriate notice, information may include candidate-submitted resume and application details, contact information, qualifications, job-related licences and availability, role preferences, and consent/contact history. Employer information may include real vacancies, hiring criteria, work contact details, and search coordination records.

We do not plan to request Social Security numbers, dates of birth, medical information, or financial account data through candidate intake. Sensitive onboarding information belongs with the hiring employer, not HireVessa.

3. Why information would be used

Only to perform authorized recruiting functions: verify job-related requirements, match willing candidates to relevant roles, coordinate consideration, support human review, maintain legally necessary records, and respond to requests. Candidate information is not sold and is not shared among sister brands for marketing.

4. Candidate consent and choices

Candidates would be told the role or accurately described employer type, the use of AI-assisted matching, and how to opt out. They would have a way to request human review, correct their record, access their personal data when applicable, and request deletion subject to lawful exceptions.

5. AI and human review

AI may assist with matching candidates against employer-confirmed, job-related criteria. Final hiring decisions remain with employers. Consequential adverse decisions are subject to human review as described in the operating model.

6. Data sharing

Role-relevant candidate details may be shared with the employer for the role to which the candidate consented to be considered. Necessary service providers would be selected and contracted before intake. No automatic transfer from RoleNestra or any other company occurs without separate, purpose-specific consent.

7. Intended retention

Candidate pool consent is planned for re-confirmation every 12 months. Per-search working records are planned for deletion 12 months after search closure unless an active pool consent or lawful retention requirement applies. Deletion requests are planned to be actioned within seven days where legally permitted; evidence needed for claims or disputes may be retained as legally required.

8. Security

Planned controls include encryption in transit and at rest, least-privilege access, administrator MFA, and access logging. No claims of independently certified security are made on this site.

9. Rights request channel

A verified privacy contact address and an authenticated rights-request workflow must be added before candidate intake begins. Do not send personal information through the prelaunch site.

10. Updates

Final effective and revision dates will appear here after legal review. Applicable rights are governed by law and are not restricted by this draft.

Refund policy · Draft

Clear remedies for real service failures.

This PRELAUNCH DRAFT explains the proposed refund principles. It requires legal and operations approval before billing is enabled.

No payments are processed here today. These provisions are a planned policy, not a currently operative refund agreement.

1. The starting principle

Refunds are designed to remedy non-delivery, material defects, missed agreed delivery windows, and compliance incidents—not buyer's remorse over properly delivered and consumed work. Nothing limits remedies required by applicable law.

2. Earned work and cancellation

Documented work already performed is generally earned. A cancellation stops future recurring charges in line with the final agreement; unused or undelivered portions are addressed under the applicable service commitments and the law.

3. No-shortlist remedy

If a completed, agreed sourcing cycle produces no candidates meeting the confirmed criteria, the employer may select a no-additional-charge criteria revision and re-run or a credit for that search slot to a different role. If the re-run also produces no shortlist, the proposed remedy includes a refund of that search's attributable subscription portion on request.

4. Compliance incidents

Affected search periods may be refunded in full on request when a verified violation of the real-role, human-review, consent, or employment boundary rules materially affected the search. Ordinary consumption limits do not extinguish statutory rights or remedies for material misconduct.

5. Disputes

Chargebacks may pause only the disputed work during review, with relevant agreement, delivery, communication and consent evidence handled securely. Unrelated services should not automatically be suspended.

6. Finalization required

Agreed sourcing-cycle durations, refund request methods, timelines, calculation methods, and verified support contact details must appear in the final policy before paid launch.
